<neutral_rules>
- **English only.** Hisham renders Arabic himself; add a register note if Arabic delivery is likely.
- **No invented claims.** Every "you run / you've spent / you co-founded" line traces to the contact's scrape capture. Never "you've talked about…" or "you said…" unless it is in their real posts. Never reference a conversation or framing that did not happen.
- **No em dashes.** Use a colon, comma, or new sentence.
- **No promises ahead of reality.** No "I'll show you", "see it working", or demo offers until the demo is live (see the cluster section for the current state).
- **Humanize.** Plain, specific sentences; no hype adjectives, no marketing voice.
- **Surgical revisions.** Change only what Hisham asks; diff before presenting.
- **Don't inflate or deflate our experience.** State what we are building and for whom, nothing more.
</neutral_rules>

<opener_structure>
Proven pattern (built on the Nabeel formula, refined through the sent Almentor and Iqraaly openers):

1. **Greeting:** "Good evening <First name>," — add "thanks for accepting." only for a DM right after a connection acceptance; never in InMail.
2. **Ego-feed opener (first, about them):** one or two sentences naming their role and why their seat makes their opinion the one wanted. Sourced only from the scrape.
3. **Personal line:** Hisham's own use of Coursera's AI companion after lectures (summaries, quizzes). Keep it short and first-person.
4. **What we're building:** the cluster pitch line (see clusters).
5. **Scoped gap claim, its own paragraph:** "I conducted some research, and no <scoped category> has shipped anything like this yet."
6. **Two-part, yes-leaning opinion question:** "Do you think something like this would add considerable value to <their company> or similar <category> platforms? And <second question matched to their remit>?"

**InMail:** first line `SUBJECT: <short topic>: your read` (stored in Message Body, Hisham pastes it into the subject field).
**Same-company threads:** vary the second question per remit (content, commercial, production, learning design, CEO) so messages don't read as a template if compared.
</opener_structure>

<follow_up_steps>
- **No-reply nudge:** short (2–3 sentences), no re-pitch, no guilt, one easy question. Default cadence 14 days after the opener; the date is always confirmed by Hisham.
- **Reply pivot (step 2):** build on their actual answer first; add regional proof if relevant (cluster section); propose a short call only when they leaned in; no demo promises until live.
- **Partner / warm check-in:** reference the last concrete thing that happened, ask the one question that unblocks the next step.
</follow_up_steps>

<register>
- **Senior warm acquaintance** (e.g. a prominent CEO/investor Hisham knows socially): respectful of her/his time, direct ask, zero flattery, no chummy phrasing ("miss you", "would love to hear your news", "no rush at all" are wrong). Not a peer tone.
- **Cold operator/manager:** the opener pattern above.
- **Existing client or partner:** plain and concrete; no pitch.
</register>
